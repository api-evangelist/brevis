Pico Prism 2.1 achieves a significant milestone by proving 99.9% of Ethereum mainnet blocks in under 10 seconds at a 60M gas limit. This new version averages 4.87 seconds for proving time, reduced by more than 20% from its predecessor. The improvements stemmed largely from AutoEng, an autonomous AI engineering agent, showcasing advancements in real-time proving efficiency.

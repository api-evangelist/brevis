Brevis Vera is now available for public use at vera.brevis.network, allowing users to capture images with C2PA-enabled devices, edit them, and generate cryptographic proof files. This tool utilizes zero-knowledge proofs to ensure privacy throughout the editing process, enabling independent verification of image provenance and edits, enhancing trust in visual media.

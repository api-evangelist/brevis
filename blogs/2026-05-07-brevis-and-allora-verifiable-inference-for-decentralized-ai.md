Brevis is collaborating with Allora to enhance decentralized AI inference with zero-knowledge verifiability. This integration allows Brevis to serve as a ZK oracle and host for Allora's loss function, enabling provable weight adjustments for worker models. The partnership ensures data authenticity, optimizing the inference process without trust dependencies, fostering secure AI applications.

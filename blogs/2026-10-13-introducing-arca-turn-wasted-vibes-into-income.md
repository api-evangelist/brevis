Arca is a new app designed to monetize AI sessions that users typically discard. By installing Arca, it scans saved sessions, anonymizes sensitive data, and allows users to choose which sessions to submit for payment in USDC. Early access is invite-only, rewarding users based on their session quality and expertise.

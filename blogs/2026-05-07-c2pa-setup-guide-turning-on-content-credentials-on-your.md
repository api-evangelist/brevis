C2PA is an open standard allowing devices to sign images at capture, ensuring verifiable authenticity. Brevis's Vera tool utilizes C2PA to track photo edits and maintain original integrity. Supported devices include the Leica M11-P, Sony Alpha series, Nikon Z6III, and Google Pixel 9 and 10, enhancing photo trustworthiness.
